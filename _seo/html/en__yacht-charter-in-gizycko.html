Find out the answers to the most frequently asked questions

  1. Is a license or certification required to rent a yacht?

    In the case of motorboats it is possible to charter without a license if the boat meets all of the following conditions:

    • hull length up to 13 m,

    • engine power up to 75 kW (which is about 102 hp),

    • maximum speed structurally limited to 15 km/h.

    All houseboats in our offer meet the conditions above, so for operating them no license is required. 

  2. What does the yacht pickup and return process look like?

    The pickup and return of the yacht are handled calmly and without rush. Before the cruise we check the technical condition and equipment of the boat together, and after the return the yacht is assessed in the same way We do not look for tiny signs of normal use - we care about a fair and trouble-free settlement of the charter.

    On the day the yacht is handed over, we also conduct a detailed. theory briefing, explain how to operate the boat and discuss safety rules. We are happy to answer questions because we want both the yacht and the crew to be well prepared and safe during the cruise.

  3. Is a deposit required, and in what form?

    Yes, a security deposit is collected when chartering a yacht, and its amount depends on the selected model, according to the booking arrangements the agreement is signed.

  4. Are the yachts well equipped and ready for the cruise?

    All yachts are very well equipped and carefully prepared for the cruise. Every boat undergoes regular technical inspections and is checked thoroughly before being handed over to the crew. We make sure the yacht is fully ready for calm and safe cruising boat.

  5. Are yachts suitable for people without much experience?

    Yes, the offer includes yachts that are also suitable for people sailing recreationally or with less experience. We help select a boat matched to the crew's skills and the character of the cruise so that sailing stays comfortable and safe.

  6. Can you take your pet with you on a yacht?

    In most cases it is possible to cruise with a pet, not only a dog, but it always requires prior agreement. We care both about the crew's comfort and about keeping the yachts in good condition, so each situation is arranged individually.

  7. What happens in the event of a breakdown or technical problem?

    During the charter we provide technical support. In the event of a breakdown or problem, the crew can contact us quickly and we help resolve the situation as efficiently and safely as possible. The yachts are serviced regularly, so serious technical problems are very rare.

Forecasts for 2026 are even more optimistic, which confirms that sailing is gaining popularity as a form of vacation available to everyone.
