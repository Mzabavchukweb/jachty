Antila 33.3 Infinitum

ANTILA 33.3

Antila 33.3 is an improved version of the popular Antila 33 and one of the largest cabin units in this segment available in Masuria. The model is distinguished by an elongated hull line, larger usable space and a refined design compared to previous versions.

It is a tourist cabin yacht designed for a comfortable charter for 8 people, with the possibility of maximum use of space with a larger crew. Maximum 10 people.

Below you will find detailed information: Antila 33.3 technical data, dimensions, interior, berths, number of people and approximate price.

Antila 33.3 - technical data

Key model parameters:

• Full length: 10.79 m
• Hull length: 9,60 m
• Full width: 3.24 m
• Draft without centreboard / with centreboard: 0.45 m / 1.80 m
• Draft with keel: 1.85 m / 1.60 m
• Maximum height in the cabin: 1.95 m
• Sail area (jib + mainsail): 51.2 m²
• Ballast: 1500 kg
• Mast height: 12,75 m
• Maximum number of crew: 10
• Number of sleeping places: 6 + 4

These parameters make the Antila 33.3 yacht stable and predictable in stronger winds. The longer overall length and solid ballast translate into a quiet behavior of the unit.

Antila 33.3 - dimensions and usable space

In terms of space, the Antila 33.3 offers more room than earlier models in this series. With a length of 10.79 m and a width of 3.24 m, it gives the crew greater comfort. A separate shower cubicle has been created in the bathroom cabin, which has a real impact on onboard comfort.

The height of 1.95 m in the cabin ensures comfort of movement even for taller people. This is a model that gives a clear feeling of more space than Antila 30.

Antila 33.3 – interior and onboard layout

Antila 33.3 interior has been designed in a modern style.

The standard layout includes:

• Lockable bow cabin
• Two aft cabins
• Spacious mess hall with additional sleeping places
• Kitchenette (galley)
• A separate toilet with a marine toilet and a shower cabin.

Antila 33.3 has a well-lit and functional interior.

Antila 33.3 – berths and crew capacity?

One of the most frequently asked questions is how many people can be accommodated on Antila 33.3.

• Maximum number of crew: 10 persons
• Number of sleeping places: 6 + 4

Real comfort is provided by a crew of 8 people. With a maximum occupancy of 10 people, the space is fully utilized but still functional during a holiday cruise.

Antila 33.3 – charter

Antila 33.3 charter is the choice for crews who expect more space and a more stable structure than models around 30 feet.

A sail area of 51.2 m² and solid 1500 kg ballast provide good handling both in moderate and stronger winds. A 21 HP inboard engine and a bow thruster make manoeuvring in harbours easier.

This is a model chosen by people who want to combine the living comfort of a larger unit, but still with a typically Masurian, tourist character.

Antila 33.3 - price

The charter price depends on the season, the standard of equipment of a specific vessel and the length of the cruise.

In the high season, rates are higher than in May, June or September. The full price list of Antila 33.3 yachts available in the offer can be found in the tab Price list.

For shorter and longer rentals, prices are determined individually.

Is Antila 33.3 a good choice?

If you are looking for a yacht:

• Modern and comfortable
• With three lockable cabins
• With a longer length and usable space
• For 6-10 people
• With stable nautical properties

Antila 33.3 will be a very reasonable and comfortable option.

It is a model that combines space, stability and modern construction, which is why it remains very popular among crews chartering yachts in Masuria.
