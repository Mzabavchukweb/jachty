Price includes:
  • insurance
  • on-the-route service in the event of breakdown through the fault of the shipowner
  • full gas cylinder
  • full fresh water tank
  • full fuel tank (the charterer must return the yacht with a refuelled fuel tank)
  • empty faecal tank (the charterer must return the yacht with an empty tank)

DISCOUNTS

  • 5% - customers sailing with us for at least two seasons
  • 5% - two-week charter
  • 5% - students in May, June and September (Monday to Thursday)

ADDITIONAL CHARGES

Mandatory:
  • Surcharge for short charters - to be agreed individually
  • After-hire cleaning (we use dedicated yacht chemicals for cleaning) - fee includes: cleaning of usable areas, vacuuming, washing the deck
  • Fee for after-hire does not include: washing dishes - 50 PLN, deck and outside lockers clearing - 50 PLN
Not mandatory:
  • Hiring a skipper: from 300 PLN / a day
  • Wypożyczenie SUPa: 200zł / tydzień
  • ONE WAY charter: from 500 PLN (fee to be determined individually)
  • Emptying the faecal tank: 250 PLN
  • Rental of bed linen: 70 PLN / set (pillow + duvet + sheet)
  • Unfuelled yacht: 200 PLN + fuel cost
  • A dog/ a cat on a yacht: 150 PLN
  • Wypożyczenie sprzętu nurkowego: od 2000zł / tydzień

All prices shown on the website are gross prices and do not include discounts.
