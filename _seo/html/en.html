Yacht Charter Mazury

For more than 10 years we have been offering yacht charter in Masuria, giving our guests a safe and relaxed time on the water. We are a local company from Giżycko focused on sound technical standards, reliability and genuine comfort for every crew.

Our fleet includes carefully prepared sailing yachts, motorboats and houseboats, regularly serviced and fully equipped. We make sure yacht hire in Masuria is straightforward and well organized, whatever your experience at the helm.

We know how valuable even a short break can be in a busy life, so we prepare every boat to make your time on the water genuinely enjoyable, without needless hassle.

We provide training before the cruise, assistance in route planning and technical support on the Great Masurian Lakes Trail. This ensures that the boat rental process runs smoothly and the crew can focus solely on relaxing.

Yacht charter in Masuria - a relaxed holiday begins on the water

There is something about Masuria that makes even a few hours on the water feel like a proper reset. Quiet lakes, the scent of the forest and evenings in the harbor are hard to compare with any other kind of holiday. That is why yacht charter suits people who want to slow down and spend time exactly as they like, without rushing and without stress.

As a local company from Giżycko with many years of experience in the sailing trade, we understand what guests really need. A holiday should be easy and enjoyable, so we look after every detail, from preparing the boat to helping you along the Great Masurian Lakes route.

Why book your charter with us?

Because we know a Masurian holiday should feel enjoyable, not like a test of patience. We keep the whole process, from booking to handover, simple and calm. That means your yacht hire runs smoothly and your crew can simply enjoy the cruise, the views and the time together.

What do you gain when choosing ours offer:

  • We offer a wide choice of yachts suited to different needs.
  • We provide professional support and a briefing before you set off.
  • We help you plan a route through the most beautiful parts of the Masurian lakes.
  • Our yacht base operates from an excellent location in Giżycko.
  • We prepare every boat to meet the expectations of even the most demanding crews.

How do booking and yacht hire work with us?

The process is simple and clear. Just choose your dates, pick the boat and make a booking by phone or email. Our sailing yacht and motorboat prices in Masuria are easy to follow, and we publish the detailed 2026 price list well in advance so you can plan your budget properly.

If this is your first cruise, there is nothing to worry about. We provide a short briefing and help with route planning, so even a beginner can feel confident at the helm very quickly.

What is in our fleet - sailing yachts, motorboats and houseboats

Our offer for this year and the coming seasons includes sailing yachts, motorboats and comfortable houseboats. That makes it easy to match your style of holiday to the way you like to travel.

The fleet includes classic family-friendly boats such as Antila models, quicker and sportier Maxus yachts, and comfortable motorboats including Futura models. We choose every sailing yacht and motorboat carefully so it delivers real comfort and stays easy to use even on longer cruises.

For guests who value comfort, we also offer well-equipped yachts and comfortable motorboats that are ideal for a calm family holiday. Hiring a boat is about more than signing a contract and collecting the keys. It is about safety, technical condition and having help when you need it. Our fleet is serviced regularly, and each boat is prepared as if it were setting off on its owners private cruise.

Do you need a license and what formalities are involved?

Many guests ask whether a sailing license is required. For sailing yachts, the right qualifications are always necessary, but several motorboats in our fleet can be driven without a motorboat license. We are happy to point you towards the best option.

If you are interested in hiring a sailing yacht or a motorboat in Masuria, we will match the right boat to your experience and travel plans.

Charter as a genuinely restful way to travel

According to travel industry research, more than 60% of people say that being close to water and nature noticeably lowers stress levels within just a few days. No wonder sailing is coming back into favor as a brilliant way to spend a holiday away from the crowds.

Spending a week on the water, stopping in small harbors and quiet bays, is a completely different experience from staying in a hotel. Many people who try sailing once return to Masuria year after year.

How do you choose the right yacht and plan your cruise?

The right boat depends on the number of people, the route you have in mind and the way you like to travel.

We will help you choose a sailing yacht or motorboat that suits your crew and your plans. That makes it easier to hire the right boat and set off with confidence.

Yacht charter in Masuria with JachtyMazury - an offer for everyone

Whether you are planning a yacht charter, a week away or a short weekend break, we have prepared a wide range of boats. Our charter offer includes sailing yachts, modern motorboats and comfortable houseboats.

For guests planning the 2026 season, we are preparing more boats, expanding the fleet and giving you even more cruising options. New models are being added to suit different ways of traveling.

Ready for an adventure?

If you dream of a yacht in Masuria and a calm holiday on the water, it is worth booking with jachtymazury.pl today and securing your dates in advance. We will gladly help you choose the right boat and plan the route so the whole trip feels properly well spent.

We offer yacht charter, advice, briefings and support at every stage of the trip. You only need to choose your dates, and we will take care of the rest so you can step aboard, cast off and let Masuria do the rest. Sometimes the best holiday plan is no plan at all and a gentle wind in the sails.
